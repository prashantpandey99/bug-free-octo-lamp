Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 Reference Guide

Statutory clauses, mandatory declarations, standard metric units, and penalty schedules.

Rule 6(1)(a)

Manufacturer / Packer / Importer

Every package must bear complete registered name and physical postal address (plot/street, city, state, 6-digit pincode) of manufacturer, packer, or importer.

Rule 6(1)(b)

Generic / Common Commodity Name

Prominent mention of the commodity's standard or generic commercial designation on the Principal Display Panel (PDP).

Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 12

Net Quantity & Standard Units

Strict SI metric units (g, kg, ml, l, N, U) without trailing full stops or pluralisation. Prohibits non-standard symbols like "gm", "gms", "kgs", "ltrs".

Rule 6(1)(d)

Month & Year of Manufacture / Packing

Clear declaration of Month and 2/4-digit Year of manufacturing, packing, or import (e.g. 08/2026 or Aug 2026), alongside expiry/best before details.

Rule 6(1)(e)

Maximum Retail Price (MRP)

Mandatory declaration in INR with explicit phrase "(inclusive of all taxes)" or "(incl. of all taxes)". Dual pricing or price alteration is strictly penalized.

Rule 6(1)(f) Amendment

Unit Sale Price (USP)

Mandatory for packages containing net quantity greater than 1 kg, 1 L, or multi-piece packs (e.g. ₹ 0.69 per g or ₹ 55.00 per kg).

Rule 6(1)(n)

Consumer Grievance Redressal

Mandatory declaration of official contact person/designation, registered postal address, active telephone / toll-free helpline, and valid email address.

Schedule II

Minimum Font Size on PDP

Prescribes minimum numeral height for Net Quantity: ≤50g (1.0mm), 50-200g (2.0mm), 200g-1kg (4.0mm), >1kg (6.0mm).

Section 36(1)

Statutory Penalties & Offenses

First Offense: Up to ₹ 25,000 fine. Second Offense: ₹ 50,000 fine. Subsequent Offenses: Up to ₹ 1,00,000 fine or imprisonment up to 1 year.
