# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 - Statutory Reference Guide

## Summary of Key Provisions for Enforcement Officers & Quality Auditors

### 1. Mandatory Declarations (Rule 6)
Every package of pre-packed commodity shall bear legible, prominent, and definite declarations on the Principal Display Panel (PDP):

| Rule Clause | Mandatory Declaration | Compliance Standard | Common Violations Observed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rule 6(1)(a)** | Name & Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer | Full postal address with street, city, state & 6-digit PIN code. | Incomplete address (only city mentioned, missing PIN code). |
| **Rule 6(1)(b)** | Common or Generic Name | Clear commercial designation of commodity. | Vague branding without descriptive commodity name. |
| **Rule 6(1)(c)** | Net Quantity | Standard metric units (`g`, `kg`, `ml`, `l`, `N`, `U`). | Use of illegal non-standard units (`gms`, `gm.`, `kgs`, `ltrs`). |
| **Rule 6(1)(d)** | Month & Year of Mfg / Packing | Format `MM/YYYY` or `Month YYYY`. | Month mentioned without year (e.g. `AUG`), illegible ink. |
| **Rule 6(1)(e)** | Maximum Retail Price (MRP) | "MRP Rs. xx.xx (inclusive of all taxes)" or "₹ xx.xx (incl. of all taxes)". | Missing mandatory tax inclusion phrase, dual price stickers. |
| **Rule 6(1)(f)** | Unit Sale Price (USP) | Required for packs $> 1\text{ kg} / 1\text{ L}$ (e.g. ₹/g, ₹/ml). | Missing Unit Sale Price on bulk/family packs. |
| **Rule 6(1)(n)** | Consumer Grievance Details | Name/Designation, Address, Helpline phone number, & Email ID. | Missing grievance email ID or non-functional telephone. |
| **Rule 6(10)** | Country of Origin | Mandatory on all pre-packed & imported commodities. | Missing country of origin on imported packaged goods. |

### 2. Principal Display Panel & Minimum Font Size (Schedule II)

| Net Quantity Range | Minimum Font Height (Normal Package) | Minimum Font Height (Blown/Moulded/Perforated) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to $50\text{ g}$ or $50\text{ ml}$ | $1.0\text{ mm}$ | $2.0\text{ mm}$ |
| $50\text{ g}$ to $200\text{ g}$ ($50\text{ ml}$ to $200\text{ ml}$) | $2.0\text{ mm}$ | $4.0\text{ mm}$ |
| $200\text{ g}$ to $1\text{ kg}$ ($200\text{ ml}$ to $1\text{ L}$) | $4.0\text{ mm}$ | $6.0\text{ mm}$ |
| Above $1\text{ kg}$ or $1\text{ L}$ | $6.0\text{ mm}$ | $8.0\text{ mm}$ |

### 3. Penal Provisions under Legal Metrology Act, 2009

- **Section 36(1)**: Penalty for manufacturing, packing, importing, selling, or distributing non-conforming pre-packaged commodities:
  - **First Offence**: Fine up to **₹ 25,000/-**
  - **Second Offence**: Fine up to **₹ 50,000/-**
  - **Subsequent Offences**: Fine from **₹ 50,000/- to ₹ 1,00,000/-** or imprisonment up to **1 year**, or both.
- **Section 36(2)**: Penalty for charging in excess of the Maximum Retail Price (MRP): Fine up to **₹ 5,000/-** (first offence) to **₹ 10,000/-** (subsequent).
- **Section 48**: Compounding of offences by Authorized Controller / Legal Metrology Officer.
